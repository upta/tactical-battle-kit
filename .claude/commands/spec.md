# /spec

Design-intent capture into SPEC.md. Interview, then write.

- One question at a time, each with a recommendation attached; never a menu
  of batched options.
- Settle, in order: what the kit is for (who consumes it, what they build),
  the bar (what a game author must be able to express without editing the
  addon), the pillars, deliberate non-goals, open questions.
- Fold answers into SPEC.md's existing sections IN PLACE. No dated layers, no
  changelog, no "Redesign (date)" stacks; rewrite the section.
- Mechanics belong in /plan tickets, not here.
- End by stopping for approval. Do not start /plan in the same turn.
